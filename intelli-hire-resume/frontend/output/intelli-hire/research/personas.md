## Persona 1: Rachel — "The Overwhelmed Recruiter"

### Demographics
- **Age**: 28-35
- **Title**: Talent Acquisition Specialist / Recruiter
- **Company size**: 50-200 employees (mid-market, scaling fast)
- **Industry**: Tech, SaaS, FinTech, E-commerce
- **Income**: $55K-$85K/year
- **Device**: 70% desktop (at work), 30% mobile (commute/evenings)
- **Tech comfort**: High — uses Slack, Notion, Google Workspace daily

### Psychology
- **Pain Points**:
  1. Drowning in 200+ resumes per open role — manually screening takes 23 hours/week
  2. Current ATS (BambooHR/basic tool) has no AI — just a database with filters
  3. Can't articulate WHY she picked one candidate over another — hiring managers ask for data she doesn't have
  4. Losing top candidates to faster-moving competitors (avg 3.5 days to respond vs. industry best of <24hrs)
- **Goals**: Screen faster, present candidates with data-backed rationale, reduce time-to-fill from 45 days to 25 days
- **Fears**: AI will replace her job; picks biased candidates without knowing it; looks unprofessional presenting "gut feeling" decisions to leadership
- **Objections** (why she might NOT buy):
  1. "We already have an ATS — why switch?" → Address: IntelliHire integrates with existing tools, or replaces with zero migration pain
  2. "AI will just auto-reject good candidates" → Address: human-in-the-loop, AI recommends but you decide
  3. "My boss won't approve the budget" → Address: ROI calculator showing hours saved → dollars saved
  4. "How accurate is the AI really?" → Address: transparent scoring with evidence, not black box

### Behavior
- **Buying Triggers**: New quarter hiring push, losing a great candidate to slow process, team complaint about recruiter bottleneck, reading LinkedIn article about AI recruiting
- **Search Behavior**: Google + G2 reviews + LinkedIn recommendations. Searches "best AI recruiting tools 2026", "resume screening automation", "ATS with AI". Reads comparison articles. Watches product demo videos.
- **Decision Timeline**: MEDIUM — 2-4 weeks research, then pushes for trial approval internally
- **Decision Authority**: Recommender (needs VP/HR Director approval for budget)

### Landing Page Implications
- **Preferred Tone**: Empathetic + empowering ("we help you look like a superhero")
- **Must-Have on LP**: Speed claims ("screen 200 resumes in 5 minutes"), accuracy stats, free trial with no CC
- **Primary CTA**: "Start Free Trial"
- **Key visual**: Dashboard showing AI scores next to candidate names — the "aha moment"

- **Device**: 50% desktop, 40% tablet (meetings), 10% mobile

- **Fears**: Implementing a tool that the team won't adopt; AI bias causing a PR incident; expensive contract with poor ROI
- **Objections**:
  1. "We're already on Greenhouse/Lever — migration is painful" → Address: import wizard, parallel running period, dedicated migration support
  2. "How do you handle bias in AI?" → Address: bias detection dashboard, explainable scoring, DEI reporting built-in
  3. "Can it handle our volume?" → Address: enterprise case studies, 99.9% uptime SLA
  4. "What's the total cost vs. Greenhouse?" → Address: transparent pricing page, TCO comparison calculator

### Behavior
- **Buying Triggers**: Annual budget planning (Q4), leadership mandate for AI adoption, DEI audit findings, renewal of existing ATS contract
- **Search Behavior**: G2/Gartner reports, peer recommendations at HR conferences (SHRM, HR Tech), vendor comparison spreadsheets. Searches "Greenhouse alternative 2026", "AI ATS with DEI reporting", "recruitment analytics platform"
- **Decision Timeline**: SLOW — 1-3 months evaluation with multiple stakeholders
- **Decision Authority**: Decision maker (budget owner), but needs CEO/CFO sign-off for $1K+/mo

### Landing Page Implications
- **Preferred Tone**: Authoritative + ROI-focused ("save $X per hire, reduce bias by Y%")
- **Must-Have on LP**: ROI metrics, DEI compliance features, enterprise logos, security certs (SOC 2), Greenhouse/Lever comparison
- **Primary CTA**: "Request a Demo" or "See ROI Calculator"

## Persona 4: Linh — "The Job Candidate"

### Demographics
- **Age**: 24-35
- **Title**: Software Engineer / Product Designer / Data Analyst (job seeker)
- **Experience**: 2-8 years
- **Device**: 60% mobile, 40% desktop
- **Tech comfort**: High
- **Location**: Global (Vietnam, India, US, EU)

### Psychology
- **Pain Points**:
  1. Applied to 50+ jobs, heard back from 3 — feels like applications go into a black hole
  2. Doesn't know why she was rejected — no feedback from AI or recruiter
  3. Resume formatting varies across platforms — some parse correctly, some don't
  4. Application forms ask the same questions already in her resume
- **Goals**: Apply quickly, know her application status in real-time, get feedback to improve
- **Fears**: AI rejecting her because of formatting/keywords, not because of actual skills
- **What she values**: Transparency, speed, respect for her time

### Application Form Implications
- **Must-Have**: Drag-drop resume upload, auto-fill from parsed CV, progress indicator, mobile-friendly
- **Nice-to-Have**: "Resume Health Score" after upload, skill gap suggestions, confirmation email with tracking link
- **Deal-breaker**: Forms that take >5 minutes, re-entering info already in resume, no confirmation after submit

### Tracking Page Implications
- **Must-Have**: Real-time status (Applied → Screening → Interview → Decision), estimated timeline
- **Nice-to-Have**: AI feedback ("Your skills match 82% of requirements"), tips to improve for future applications
