## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Do
- Be specific with numbers ("2.3 seconds", "98.5%", "200 resumes")
- Use active voice ("Screen faster" not "Screening is accelerated")
- Address the person directly ("your pipeline", "your candidates")
- Show confidence without arrogance ("the AI that shows its work")
- Acknowledge candidate experience as a feature, not an afterthought

### Don't
- Use jargon without explanation ("NLP-powered semantic matching" → "AI that understands skills")
- Overpromise ("replace your entire HR team" → "make your team 3x more productive")
- Use fear ("you'll lose candidates without AI" → "your best candidates are waiting")
- Be vague ("smart hiring" → "screen 200 resumes in 5 minutes")
- Ignore the candidate perspective (always include both recruiter AND candidate value)
