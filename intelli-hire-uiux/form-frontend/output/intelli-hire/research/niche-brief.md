## Key Market Trends

### 1. Cloud/SaaS Dominance
- 73% of HR SaaS is cloud-based (2025)
- On-premise declining to <28%
- SaaS = lower barrier to entry for SMBs

### 2. AI Integration is Table Stakes
- Resume parsing, candidate matching, chatbot screening are baseline expectations
- Differentiators: explainable AI, bias detection, predictive analytics
- "Assisted AI" (human+AI) preferred over "fully autonomous"

### 3. Trust & Transparency Gap
- Only 26% of applicants trust AI to evaluate them fairly
- Explainable AI scoring = competitive advantage
- Human oversight is expected, not optional

### 4. Regulation Pressure
- EU AI Act (Aug 2026) — compliance requirements for hiring AI
- Algorithmic bias concerns — 30%+ companies cite ethical worries
- Opportunity: "fair AI" positioning

### 5. Platform Consolidation
- ServiceNow acquired Moveworks for $2.9B (Mar 2025)
- Trend: all-in-one suites (payroll + analytics + hiring + EX)
- Counter-opportunity: focused best-of-breed tools for specific workflows

## Key Market Insights

1. **Timing is perfect**: AI recruitment adoption doubled in one year (26% → 43%). By 2026, 80%+ enterprises will use AI in hiring. IntelliHire enters a market that's rapidly growing but not yet consolidated.

2. **Trust gap = opportunity**: Only 26% of candidates trust AI in hiring. IntelliHire's "shows its work" positioning directly addresses this — explainable scores, visible matching logic, human oversight.

3. **SMB underserved**: Most AI recruitment tools target enterprise ($2K+/mo). SMBs and mid-market teams (1-500 employees) are price-sensitive but actively looking for AI tools. Freemium + affordable pricing wins here.

4. **Regulation as moat**: EU AI Act compliance (Aug 2026) will force vendors to show algorithmic transparency. IntelliHire's built-in explainability becomes a compliance advantage, not just a UX feature.

5. **Resume parsing is commodity, matching is gold**: Parsing accuracy is 95%+ across vendors. The differentiator is what you DO with parsed data — matching quality, skill gap analysis, predictive scoring. This is where IntelliHire must excel.

6. **Candidate experience matters**: Best-in-class platforms (Paradox, Greenhouse) invest heavily in candidate-facing UX. A beautiful apply form + transparent tracking page = higher application completion + employer brand boost.
