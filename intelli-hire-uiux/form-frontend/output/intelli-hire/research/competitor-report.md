## Cross-Competitor Patterns

### Table Stakes (EVERYONE does — IntelliHire MUST have)
1. **AI-powered features** prominently mentioned (parsing, matching, screening)
2. **"Request a demo" or "Start free trial"** as primary CTA
3. **Integration ecosystem** (job boards, HRIS, calendars, Slack)
4. **Customer logos** or social proof numbers above the fold
5. **Responsive design** with mobile-first considerations
6. **Security certifications** (SOC 2, ISO 27001)

### Differentiators (only 1-2 do — potential advantage)
1. **Free trial with no credit card** — only Workable does this → IntelliHire should too
2. **Dark/distinctive color palette** — only Lever uses dark mode → opportunity for IntelliHire
3. **Fraud detection** (Greenhouse "Real Talent") — unique feature → IntelliHire could add AI quality signals
4. **Candidate-facing portal** (Greenhouse "MyGreenhouse") — rare → IntelliHire tracking page
5. **Specific time-to-hire reduction claim** — Greenhouse cites 30% → IntelliHire needs its own metric

### Gaps (NOBODY does — IntelliHire's biggest opportunity)

1. **🔥 No AI transparency/explainability**
   - None of the 3 competitors explain HOW their AI scores candidates
   - No "why this score" breakdown visible anywhere
   - IntelliHire: show match reasoning, skill weights, confidence levels
   - **This is our #1 differentiator**

2. **🔥 No candidate-facing AI feedback**
   - Candidates submit and wait in the dark
   - No competitor shows candidates their AI match score or improvement tips
   - IntelliHire: give candidates a "Resume Health Score" after applying

3. **No bold visual design**
   - Workable: safe corporate green/white
   - Greenhouse: premium dark green (best of three)
   - Lever: dark but sparse
   - IntelliHire: dark obsidian + electric cyan = command-center feel = unforgettable

4. **No interactive demo on landing page**
   - All 3 require you to "request demo" or "start trial" before seeing the product
   - IntelliHire: show an interactive AI scoring demo directly on the landing page

5. **Weak empty/loading states**
   - All 3 show generic loading spinners
   - IntelliHire: scanning animations, AI processing visuals, progress indicators with personality

6. **No real-time pipeline analytics**
   - Dashboard stats are static/basic
   - IntelliHire: real-time pipeline velocity, AI-predicted time-to-fill, bottleneck detection

- Free trial CTA (no credit card) — copy Workable's lowest friction model

### Should Have (from differentiators to adopt)

### Must Exploit (from gaps — our competitive moat)

- **Candidate AI feedback** — "Resume Health Score" visible to applicants
- **Interactive landing page demo** — live AI scoring without signup
- **Command-center aesthetic** — dark obsidian + cyan = visually unforgettable
- **Real-time analytics** — pipeline velocity, AI predictions, bottleneck alerts
